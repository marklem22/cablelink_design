# Cablelink Client Dependencies

The standalone prototype must not present any item below as a completed production integration until the client supplies and approves the required information.

## Legal and company content

- Final Privacy Policy / Privacy Notice.
- Final privacy-popup wording.
- Final application consent wording.
- Final Auto Debit authorization and consent wording.
- Confirmed company, office, billing, and footer contact details.

## Official links

- Facebook page URL.
- Messenger URL.
- Viber contact or deep link.
- X (Twitter) URL for advisories.
- Production payment portal, GCash, and Maya destinations.

Public external links belong in `assets/js/site-config.js`. Secrets must not be placed in frontend code.

## Application and availability

- Authoritative coverage / service-availability data source.
- Required application document list.
- Approved upload file types and maximum file size.
- MIS/CRM endpoint, authentication, payload mapping, document mapping, and response contract.
- Duplicate-submission and failure-handling requirements.

The current prototype does not submit applications or claim that a record was created in MIS/CRM.

## Auto Debit

- Confirmed form fields.
- Confirmation that an online application is legally sufficient.
- Secure processing destination and authentication.
- Server-side validation, encryption, retention, masking, and audit requirements.

The current prototype does not transmit or store Auto Debit information.

## Other integrations and assets

- Chatbot vendor, embed/configuration, allowed pages, escalation path, and privacy requirements.
- September, October, and November campaign copy, dates, offers, and responsive artwork.
