Payment instructions

Over-the-counter
  1. Subscriber/s should bring their Service Invoice or ask the teller for " BILLS PAYMENT FORM" then indicate the required details such as ( Account number, Account name, and amount to be paid.
  2. Review all the details.
  3. Subscriber/s can settle the balance through CASH or DATED CHEQUE.
  4. Wait for the validation on your invoice or Bills payment form.
  5. Check if validated details are all correct before leaving the counter.
Online payment
  1. Go to Bayad Online: https://www.online.bayad.com
  2. Log in to your Bayad Online account.
  3. On the 'BILLS PAYMENT' section, click 'Cable/Internet' then "CABLELINK".
  4. Fill out the required details and then review before payment. Tip: Enable the SAVE BILLER feature for your next payment.
  5. Wait for your payment confirmation receipt and you're done!.

Helpful Reminders

General Payment Reminders

A few important notes to keep in mind whichever payment channel you choose.

Over-the-Counter Payment

Always bring your Statement of Account (SOA) when paying through accredited payment centers and banks.

Available at Bayad Center, 7-Eleven, and GPRS.

Credit Card Payment — Two Ways to Pay

  • Auto Debit Arrangement — Subscribers may enroll at any Cablelink satellite office.
  • Over-the-Counter — Credit card payment is accepted at selected Cablelink offices. Visa, Mastercard, AMEX, and JCB are accepted.

e-Wallets & Digital Payments

GCash, Maya, ShopeePay, GrabPay, Peppermint, Bayad Online, Coins.ph, Sold, JuanPay, Diskartech, Juan Cash, WeChat Pay, and BaryaCard.

Cheque Payments

Please make your cheque payable to Cable Link and Holdings Corp.

Pay Without Your SOA

You can conveniently pay without your SOA at Cablelink satellite offices, banks, payment centers, and digital platforms.

Crediting of Payments

Payments made through payment centers, banks, and credit cards will be credited to your account after 3 working days.
